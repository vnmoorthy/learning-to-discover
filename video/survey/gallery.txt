Three-body: the figure-eight, rediscovered
IMAGE
threebody_gpu + matplotlib

Correctness check for the new astrophysics problem (periodic orbits of the Newtonian three-body problem). Our GPU search (300,000 random initial velocities, batched Levenberg-Marquardt) converged on Moore's figure-eight: vx=0.3471168902, vy=0.5327249445, T=6.3259139921, return error 7.0e-10 in the official float64 verifier. Rediscovery of a known orbit (Moore 1993; Chenciner-Montgomery 2000).

Three-body: Moth II
IMAGE
threebody_gpu + matplotlib

Rediscovery of the 'moth II' orbit of Suvakov and Dmitrasinovic (2013): equal masses, T=28.6693, return error 1.6e-11. Found by the same GPU test run (seed 0, 300,000 seeds, periods 5 to 30) and matched to the literature catalog by initial condition and period.

Three-body: a k=5 satellite of the figure-eight
IMAGE
threebody_gpu + matplotlib

Equal masses, vx=0.1388980233, vy=0.5375024150, T=23.3346, return error 9.6e-12. Its topological word is the fifth power of the figure-eight word, so the verifier files it under the figure-eight family (a 'topological-power satellite', a known phenomenon: Suvakov 2014), not as a new family.

Three-body: candidate orbit at m3 = 1.5 (not in our catalog)
IMAGE
threebody_gpu + matplotlib

Unequal masses (m1=m2=1, m3=1.5): vx=0.3971945789, vy=0.8898238352, T=22.50016117, return error 4.7e-12 (confirmed independently with scipy DOP853: 8.8e-12). Its topological word matches none of the 2060 literature orbits we checked (Suvakov-Dmitrasinovic 2013, Li-Liao 2017, Li-Jing-Liao 2018). CANDIDATE only: the later catalogs (Li-Li-Liao 2021, Hristov et al. 2021-2024) were not checked and no multi-precision confirmation was run.
